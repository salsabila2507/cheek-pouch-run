## Product and distinctiveness

Side-scrolling auto-run, double jump, hold-to-duck, seed arcs, mushroom traps, low-flying owls, three-hit spill system, forty-seed pouch, extraction and real-block burrow encounters. The source already contains the product, tests and original procedural pixel art. Improve it where tests or real-device play reveal weaknesses. Do not replace it with a marketing landing page.

## Non-negotiable Live policy
- Poll only the documented public `https://api.squeekchain.com/v1/status` about every 5s, with timeout/backoff. Read schema v1 and reject missing/invalid/old/future/nonhardware/unhealthy data.
- Real ACTIVE + positive RPM opens scoring. RPM drives collision speed. At >=70 RPM, ZOOMIES doubles pickups.
- IDLE/zero RPM locks seeds/scored distance and raises trap pressure. Existing earned pouch can be banked by retreating.
- First observation is baseline. Forward observed projected block advance creates one golden burst and physical bank checkpoint, regardless of gap. Never replay duplicates or catch up on reconnect, pause, menu, epoch or installation change.
- Distinguish `builder.projected.blockHeight` from `builder.anchor.blockHeight`. No claims of Ethereum-finalized blocks or issued SQK from projection.
- Connection loss/unknown telemetry freezes the entire Live simulation. Absolutely no random/mock/simulated fallback in Live.
- Practice is labeled SIMULATED with separate local saves, and cannot enter Live records.
