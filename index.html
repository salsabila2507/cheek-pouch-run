FIELD NOTES ↙
01 / THE GREED
◒

Fill those cheeks.

Jump mushrooms. Duck owls. At 30 seeds, heavy cheeks slow you down. At 36+, pressure builds: hold Duck to steady or bank before you stumble and spill a quarter. Three hits end the run.

02 / THE WHEEL
✳

His pace. Your problem.

Real RPM drives your speed. At 70+ RPM, ZOOMIES doubles every pickup. Sleeping? Enter the slower night forest, dodge more owls and mushrooms, and chase survival goals. Seeds and run metres rest with him.

03 / THE WAY HOME
⌂

Know when to burrow.

A newly observed block brings gold and a burrow. Land there to bank, shed the weight and keep running. Complete local banking goals, or head home to save every seed and end your run.

A real connection. Not a costume.

Live reads Squeektoshi's hardware telemetry every ~5 seconds. Sleeping is playable: survive the night without earning new seeds or run metres. No signal? Enter a clearly labelled waiting room; the run freezes until healthy telemetry returns. No secret simulation. New projected blocks are observed once, never replayed on reconnect. Seeds are local game points, not SQK.

What we actually read: measurement.rpmMilli, coreStatus, freshness, hardware provenance, oracle health, and builder.projected.blockHeight from the public status API ↗. We validate source timestamps and reject unknown data.

Projected ≠ settled. Projected blocks include accepted wheel work that may not yet be settled on Ethereum Sepolia. The settled height is shown separately. Checkpoints are game events, not transactions or issued tokens.

Your device, your records. Live and Practice records are saved separately in this browser. There is no wallet, public leaderboard, server verification or monetary reward. Run metres are game distance, not the hamster's measured distance.
